# Weinreb et al. 2016 — RNA evolutionary couplings

- **Research question:** Can global sequence covariation recover RNA 3D contacts
  and amino-acid/nucleotide contacts in RNA–protein complexes?
- **Data:** RNA-family and RNA–protein paired alignments plus structures.
- **Method:** Maximum-entropy global probability models of nucleotide and
  amino-acid covariation.
- **Loss:** Regularized global-model likelihood/pseudolikelihood as specified by
  the implementation.
- **Evaluation:** Contact enrichment and structure prediction in RNA and
  RNA–protein examples.
- **Reproducible resources:** Paper metadata and public preprint record.
- **Direct use here:** Conceptual basis for Cas13/direct-repeat paired coupling
  and structure-based validation.
- **Not directly transferable:** Operon pairing, repeat orientation, subtype
  stratification, and Cas13 length/depth differ substantially.
- **Key risks:** Insufficient effective pairs, indirect/phylogenetic effects,
  pairing ambiguity, alignment columns with gaps, and circular validation.
