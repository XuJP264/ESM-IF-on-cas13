# Liu et al. 2017 — Cas13a architecture

- **Research question:** How does target binding activate Cas13a RNA cleavage?
- **Data:** LbuCas13a ternary crystal structure, binary cryo-EM structure, and
  biochemical evidence.
- **Method:** Structural comparison and functional characterization.
- **Loss:** Not applicable.
- **Evaluation:** RNA-channel architecture, conformational movement, HEPN
  activation, and cleavage assays.
- **Reproducible resources:** Publisher/PubMed metadata and RCSB entry 5XWP.
- **Direct use here:** Cas13a benchmark scaffold, RNA contact regions, HEPN
  manual review, and state comparison.
- **Not directly transferable:** Cas13a domain layout and residue numbering do
  not transfer position-wise to Cas13d or other subtypes.
- **Key risks:** Subtype mixing, chain/mapping errors, and overclaiming contact
  or inverse-folding recovery as preserved activity.
