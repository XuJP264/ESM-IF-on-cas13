# High-precision Cas13d for allele-specific MYH7 suppression

- Research question: whether a CasRx variant can improve allele-selective MYH7 suppression.
- Data: guide-dependent cellular and mouse phenotypes.
- Methods: variant engineering and allele-specific RNA-targeting assays.
- Loss: not a machine-learning study.
- Evaluation: assay- and guide-specific knockdown/phenotype measurements.
- Reproducible resources: open PMC article.
- Direct use here: hpCas13d substitutions and a separate qualitative activity group.
- Not directly transferable: organismal/cellular endpoints are not comparable to purified cleavage fractions.
- Key risks: target and guide specificity, tissue context, and qualitative extraction in this dataset version.
