# CasRx and DjCas13d cryo-EM structures and mutagenesis

- Research question: how CasRx and DjCas13d structures explain guide/target recognition and cleavage behavior.
- Data: apo, binary, and multiple ternary cryo-EM structures plus a CasRx biochemical mutation panel.
- Methods: cryo-EM, purified cis/trans cleavage assays, deletion/insertion design, and clustered alanine substitutions.
- Loss: not a trained predictive model.
- Evaluation: state-specific structural comparisons and WT-normalized cis/trans assay figures.
- Reproducible resources: open PMC article, Europe PMC public supplement, and RCSB 9M-series coordinates.
- Direct use here: DjCas13d/CasRx state atlas, catalytic construct restoration, and the only numerical same-assay retrospective panel in version 1.
- Not directly transferable: approximate graph readings are not raw replicates; indels cannot be scored on an unchanged backbone.
- Key risks: RCSB mutation declarations omit the paper-reported catalytic alanine constructs, numerical sample size is small, and assay-selected variants create ascertainment bias.
