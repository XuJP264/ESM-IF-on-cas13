# Zhang et al. 2018 — Cas13d structure

- **Research question:** How do apo, crRNA-bound, and target-bound Cas13d states
  support RNA-guided ribonuclease activation?
- **Data:** Cryo-EM reconstructions plus biochemical/cellular characterization.
- **Method:** Structural comparison, hydrogen–deuterium exchange, and functional
  assays.
- **Loss:** Not applicable; this is an experimental structural study.
- **Evaluation:** Resolution/QC, conformational states, RNA interactions, and
  functional perturbations.
- **Reproducible resources:** Public manuscript and RCSB entries including 6E9F.
- **Direct use here:** Cas13d chain/state labels, RNA contacts, HEPN/manual
  annotations, and multi-state constraints.
- **Not directly transferable:** Missing density is not a designable backbone;
  one subtype/state cannot represent all Cas13.
- **Key risks:** Assembly/chain selection, sequence-coordinate mapping, missing
  residues, and interpreting proximity as functional proof.
