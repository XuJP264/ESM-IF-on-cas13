# High-fidelity Cas13 variants

- Research question: whether Cas13d substitutions can retain target degradation while reducing collateral effects.
- Data: CasRx variants across purified and cell-based guide/target assays.
- Methods: structure-informed mutagenesis and assay-specific screening.
- Loss: not a trained predictive model.
- Evaluation: target knockdown and collateral effects, with experiment-specific controls.
- Reproducible resources: official article page and public supplementary PDF.
- Direct use here: exact hfCas13d substitution string and qualitative retrospective phenotype group.
- Not directly transferable: qualitative cell phenotypes cannot be pooled with purified cis/trans cleavage fractions.
- Key risks: guide, expression system, and endpoint dependence; raw replicate values were not reconstructed in version 1.
