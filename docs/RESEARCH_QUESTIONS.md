# Research Questions

1. At matched mutation rate, does constrained ESM-IF1 preserve catalytic,
   conserved, and RNA-interacting regions better than unconstrained ESM-IF1?
2. Does explicit RNA atomic context improve recovery and conditional scores at
   direct-contact and second-shell residues relative to protein-only models?
3. Do conservation, RNA contacts, and validated protein–direct-repeat
   coevolution provide complementary constraints without eliminating novelty?
4. Do masks built from multiple experimental conformational states reduce
   state-specific sequence compatibility failures?
5. Which Cas13 subtypes, structures, and structural regions show systematic
   zero-shot model failures that could justify domain adaptation?
6. How sensitive are findings to sequence weighting, cluster isolation,
   novelty matching, mask thresholds, structure state, and random seed?
