# Research Goal

Develop and validate a Cas13 inverse-folding framework that combines structural,
evolutionary, and RNA-interface constraints while retaining existing Cas13
backbones. The framework should generate statistically novel sequences, protect
HEPN catalytic regions, crRNA recognition, RNA interfaces, and important
conformational coupling regions, and compare all methods on matched scaffolds,
design positions, and novelty ranges.

The project first benchmarks experimentally resolved Cas13 complexes. It then
builds a subtype-aware natural sequence and operon-level direct-repeat resource
from CRISPR-Cas Atlas. Large-scale predicted structures and domain adaptation
are deferred until GPU/HPC execution, but all exchange interfaces must be
complete and fixture-tested.

Computational evidence stops at Level 3. Only future wet-lab validation can
support Level 4 or the phrase "effective Cas13".
