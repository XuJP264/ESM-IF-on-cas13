## Statistics and failure funnel

Seed block, not candidate, is the paired unit (`n=2`). The complete 112-row
statistics table includes paired mean differences, 2,000-replicate bootstrap
intervals, exact sign-flip p-values, and Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment for 14
metrics across eight comparisons to unconstrained ESM-IF1. Every adjusted
p-value is 1.0; these are explicitly low-power descriptive results and do not
identify a method winner. Missing Atlas coverage hits reduce the usable paired
count for that metric and are not imputed as extreme novelty.

The formal funnel contains 36 real proposals, 36 inside the common interval,
18 selected and Level-2 scored, 18 fixed-zero-violation candidates, 17
low-complexity passes, 18 homopolymer passes, 15 Atlas coverage hits, and 14
Level-1 passes. Four selected candidates failed Level 1: three because no Atlas
hit reached 80% query coverage (fail closed), and one because of a
low-complexity window. The 18 eligible but unselected proposals are also in
`failure_analysis.csv`.
