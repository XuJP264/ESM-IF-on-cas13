# Limitations

- Inverse-folding compatibility is not biochemical function.
- Available experimental Cas13 structures are few, subtype-imbalanced, and
  state-dependent.
- Missing residues and unresolved regions limit design and evaluation.
- Atlas annotations, operon pairing, repeat orientation, and taxonomy can be
  incomplete or ambiguous.
- Sequence similarity and cluster thresholds do not remove every evolutionary
  dependency.
- MI is affected by phylogeny and entropy and is not direct coupling analysis.
- Full-length protein–RNA DCA may be computationally underpowered or infeasible.
- ESM-IF1 lacks RNA atomic context and constrained sampling remains causal.
- ProteinMPNN/LigandMPNN score scales are not directly interchangeable.
- Structure prediction can recycle training-set information and remains
  computational evidence only.
- The local GPU has 8188 MiB, limiting batches and some large models.
