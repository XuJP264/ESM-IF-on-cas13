# Roadmap

1. **M0 — Governance and repository:** reproducible package, CLI, workflow,
   documentation, CI, and fixture tests.
2. **M1 — Environments and assets:** audited machine, four isolated
   environments, pinned third parties, local checkpoints, and genuine smokes.
3. **M2 — Experimental structures:** RCSB Cas13 complexes, strict QC, contact
   regions, real inverse-folding/model benchmarks, and reports.
4. **M3 — Atlas:** official v1.0 streaming pipeline, Cas13 funnel, deduplication,
   MMseqs2 clusters, leakage-safe splits, and data card.
5. **M4 — Evolution:** subtype MSAs, conservation, paired direct repeats,
   MI/APC, formal DCA integration, and structural validation.
6. **M5 — Constrained generation:** hard/soft/free masks, causal constrained
   ESM-IF1, matched baselines, LigandMPNN, novelty, diversity, and ablations.
7. **M6 — Refolding and migration:** deterministic job exchange, prediction
   ingestion, structure comparisons, GPU/HPC bundles, and mock E2E.
8. **Future:** large-scale predictions and domain adaptation only when
   preregistered evidence justifies them; Level 4 requires separate wet-lab
   validation outside this repository's current scope.
